The system

Trading Lab is built to evaluate discretionary and systematic futures strategies against the constraints traders actually face in prop firm accounts.

The application joins a FastAPI backend and SolidJS interface with a C++17 simulation engine exposed through pybind11. Historical market data moves from Databento into parquet and PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB for faster replay and backtest workflows.

  • Rule presets model drawdown and account constraints.
  • Replay keeps chart study and execution in one workflow.
  • The compiled engine handles simulation-heavy work.
  • The data pipeline reduces repeated historical sync costs.
