Soccer Analysis

Computer-vision tracking for players, movement, and match footage.

Role
Computer-vision engineering
Format

Stack
Python, YOLOv8, OpenCV

Video in, analysis out

The command-line workflow takes soccer footage and returns a processed video with player and ball tracking information drawn directly over the match.

The analysis includes possession, speed and distance information, player identification, and movement paths.

  • YOLOv8 player and ball detection
  • OpenCV tracking overlays
  • Speed and distance estimation
  • Video-in, video-out command-line flow
